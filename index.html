How to win in three steps

  1. PlaceTake turns putting your 9 pieces on the board’s 24 points.
  2. MoveSlide pieces along the lines. Line up three to form a mill.
  3. CaptureEach mill removes an enemy piece. Leave them with two to win.

Questions, answered

What is Navakankari?

It is the Indian name for Nine Men’s Morris, a two-player strategy game where you form lines of three, called mills, to capture pieces.

Is it really free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser with no account needed.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes. Choose “Two players” and share one screen, taking turns.

What is flying?

When a player is down to three pieces they may jump to any empty point. You can switch this rule off in Settings.
